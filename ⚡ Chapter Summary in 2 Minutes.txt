⚡ Chapter Summary in 2 Minutes

Electricity is a form of energy that powers devices like bulbs, fans, TVs, computers, and trains. To make electricity flow, we need a complete circuit. The chapter explains the basic parts of an electric circuit and how they work together.

🔥 Most Important Concepts
1. Electric Cell
A portable source of electrical energy.
Has two terminals:
Positive (+) terminal
Negative (−) terminal
Provides energy for current to flow.
Remember:

Metal cap = Positive (+)
Flat bottom = Negative (−)

2. Battery
A battery is a combination of two or more cells connected together.
Gives more electrical energy than a single cell.

Formula to remember:

Battery = Two or more cells

3. Electric Lamp (Bulb)
Incandescent Bulb
Contains a thin wire called a filament.
When current flows, the filament becomes hot and glows.
LED Lamp
Does not have a filament.
Uses less electricity and lasts longer.
4. Electric Circuit

An electric circuit is a complete path through which electric current flows.

Example:

Cell → Wire → Bulb → Wire → Cell

If the path is complete → Bulb glows 💡

If the path is broken → Bulb does not glow ❌

5. Switch

A switch is used to:

Complete a circuit (ON)
Break a circuit (OFF)
Switch ON  → Current flows → Bulb glows
Switch OFF → No current → Bulb off
6. Conductors and Insulators
Conductors

Materials that allow electricity to pass through.

Examples:

Copper
Aluminium
Iron
Insulators

Materials that do not allow electricity to pass through.

Examples:

Rubber
Plastic
Wood
